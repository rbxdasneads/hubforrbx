Arsenal Script – FAQ

How do I use a Arsenal script?

Copy the script, open your Roblox executor (for example Solara, Velocity or Xeno), join Arsenal, paste the code into the executor and press Execute.

What does "keyless" mean?

A keyless script runs right away. Scripts marked "Key System" ask you to get a free key from the developer's website first.
