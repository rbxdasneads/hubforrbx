Universal Roblox Scripts – FAQ

How do I use a universal Roblox script?

Copy the script, open your Roblox executor, join any game, paste the code into the executor and press Execute.

What does "keyless" mean?

A keyless script runs right away. Scripts marked "Key System" ask you to get a free key from the developer's website first.

Are these universal Roblox scripts free?

Yes. All 30 scripts on this page are free community scripts collected from ScriptBlox. Patched scripts are removed automatically.

Can I get banned for using scripts?

Yes. Using scripts breaks the Roblox Terms of Use and can get your account banned. Many players test scripts on an alternate account.
