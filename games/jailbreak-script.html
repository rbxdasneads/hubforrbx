Jailbreak Script – FAQ

How do I use a Jailbreak script?

Copy the script, open your Roblox executor, join Jailbreak, paste the code into the executor and press Execute.

What does "keyless" mean?

A keyless script runs right away. Scripts marked "Key System" ask you to get a free key from the developer's website first.
